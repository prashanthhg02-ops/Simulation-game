# Little Acre

A cozy, browser-based farm simulation game. Plant crops, water your garden, advance the day, and sell your harvest to grow your farm.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation or build step is required.

## How to play

- Choose a crop in the seed shop, then select an empty garden bed to plant it.
- Select a growing crop to water it. Crops grow when watered before ending the day.
- On rainy days, the rain waters crops for you.
- Select a ready crop to harvest it and earn coins.
- Buy more seeds and spend coins to unlock additional garden beds.
- Use **End day & rest** to advance the day and restore your energy.

Your farm saves automatically in the browser on your device. **New farm** resets your progress.
